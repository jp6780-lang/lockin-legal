Common Questions

Does LockIn include study documents?

No. The app starts empty. You upload or scan your own documents and create your own categories.

Can I upload from Google Drive?

Yes, if Google Drive is available through the Apple Files app. Install Google Drive, enable it in Files, then choose Drive when uploading documents.

Does LockIn send my PDFs to a server?

LockIn is local-first. Your documents are stored on your device unless you export, share, back up, or enable iCloud sync (which syncs through your own private Apple/iCloud account, not a developer-hosted server).

Does the AI feature cost extra?

AI features are optional. You provide your own API key and are responsible for your own AI provider's usage and billing.

How does the Friends feature work?

You invite a specific friend with a link you send yourself. Once connected, you can send each other study challenges and messages, synced through your own private iCloud accounts.
